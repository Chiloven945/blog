## Why a long-form test

This article exercises the body renderer. It contains several sections, nested headings, lists, a
blockquote, and code, so the table of contents, reading time, and responsive layout can be checked
against a realistic length.

The reading column is kept within a comfortable measure. On desktop the body and the table of
contents sit side by side; on mobile the contents collapse.

### The reading experience

Good typography should let the reader forget the typography. Line height, paragraph spacing, and
heading hierarchy should feel natural rather than relying on decoration for rhythm.

- consistent spacing between paragraphs;
- clear list indentation;
- distinct heading levels;
- code that scrolls horizontally without breaking the page.

## Content structure

An article is usually made of several parts. The sections below produce enough entries for the table
of contents.

### Introduction

The introduction establishes context. It should not be too long, and it should not hand over the
conclusion.
